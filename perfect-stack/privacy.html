Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy describes how Detektive Games handles information in Perfect Stack (Android package com.detektivegames.perfectstack).

Information collected and shared

Perfect Stack works offline and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Detektive Games or third parties. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score, sound preference, and current run, including its score and tower layout, in the app's private storage on your device. This information lets you resume a run and keep your settings. It is not sent to us or stored on our servers. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Retention and deletion

Local game information stays on your device until it is overwritten through gameplay, you clear the app's storage in Android settings, or you uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes saved runs, preferences, and best scores. We cannot recover this information.

Permissions and external services

The current Android release requests no Android permissions. Opening the privacy-policy link launches your browser and visits this website; the browser and website host process that visit under their own policies. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Google Play may separately process store, installation, and diagnostic information under Google's Privacy Policy; the game itself does not send us that information.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us. Email is processed by our email provider, Gmail, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support email, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Any future features that change how information is handled will be reflected in this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Detektive Games at detektivegames.support@gmail.com.
